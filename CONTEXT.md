# Focus Timer

A single-user, browser-only timer that alternates focused work with breaks in a fixed rhythm.

## Language

**Phase**:
One timed block of the rhythm: a Focus Session, a Short Break, or a Long Break.
_Avoid_: Interval, period, round

**Focus Session**:
A 25-minute Phase of focused work.
_Avoid_: Pomodoro, work period, sprint

**Short Break**:
A 5-minute Phase that follows a Completed Focus Session, except every fourth one.
_Avoid_: Rest

**Long Break**:
A 15-minute Phase that follows every fourth Completed Focus Session.

**Completed Focus Session**:
A Focus Session whose time ran out. A skipped Focus Session is not completed.
_Avoid_: Done pomodoro

**Skip**:
Ending the current Phase early and loading the next one.

**Reset**:
Restarting the current Phase at its full length, paused.
